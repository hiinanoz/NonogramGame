Your game stays on your device. Catgrid has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your solved puzzles, best times, collected cards, saved games and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Catgrid shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model, and ad interaction data. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also allow or deny tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Catgrid Premium subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Game Center and sharing

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and scores (daily streak, puzzles solved, Cat Puzzle levels) are sent to Apple's leaderboards and processed by Apple under its own privacy policy. You can sign out in iOS Settings → Game Center. When you share a solved puzzle or a card, the image is created on your device and sent only to the app you choose; we receive nothing.

Children

Catgrid is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Third parties

Catgrid uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies that provide the same or greater protection as this policy:

• Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform: showing ads and asking for consent (Google Privacy Policy).
• Apple: App Store purchases and, if you choose to share them in iOS settings, anonymous crash reports (Apple Privacy Policy).

We do not have our own servers or analytics, and we do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or delete the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention rules.

Your rights (EEA, UK and US states)

Personalised ads are only shown with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Non-personalised ads rely on Google's legitimate interest in delivering ads and preventing fraud. You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Residents of US states with privacy laws can opt out of personalised advertising in the same menu where it is shown. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are fulfilled by Google; write to us and we will help.

Your choices

You can delete all game progress at any time with Settings → Reset Progress, or by deleting the app. Because we keep no data on servers, there is nothing else to delete on our side. For data collected by Google for advertising, use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (where available) or iOS tracking settings.

Who we are and contact

Catgrid is developed and published by an independent developer, shown as the seller on the App Store page. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. For any privacy question or request, write to mobile@othinks.com.
