Thank you for reviewing Catgrid Collection: Nonogram.

ABOUT THE PREVIOUS REJECTION (Guideline 5.6)
An earlier build mistakenly contained a hidden developer screen for checking ad loading status. It has been completely removed, together with its code. The app has no hidden, remote-controlled or review-only features. It has no server, no remote configuration and no feature flags: every puzzle, card and mode is bundled in the binary and works offline, identically for every user, region and date. Some content unlocks only by playing (described below). The steps below reach every feature in a few minutes, without purchasing.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
- No account or login. Progress is stored on the device.
- Home screen shows the three game modes: Cat Puzzle (large card), Daily Puzzle and Card Collection.
1. Cat Puzzle: tap the Cat Puzzle card. A seasonal adventure map opens; tap Play. Rule: place one cat in every color, row and column; cats may not touch. Tap a cell to mark X, double-tap to place a cat. Level 1 is a short guided tutorial. The Easy/Hard switch (top right of the game) controls automatic X's. 335 levels, unlocked in order.
2. Daily Puzzle: tap Daily Puzzle, then Play. One new nonogram per day; the last 10 days can also be played from the calendar.
3. Card Collection (nonograms): tap Card Collection, then Continue. Muffin's School teaches nonograms; it can be skipped with "Skip tutorial". Cat breeds unlock in order: each next breed opens once half of the previous breed's puzzles are solved. Finishing all puzzles of a breed awards its collectible card (shown in Collection and on the home screen).
4. Sharing: after solving a puzzle, or when viewing a card, tap the share button (top right).
5. Game Center: the Game Center sign-in appears after launch; leaderboards are in Settings → Game Center Leaderboards.
6. Animated cats on the home screen can be petted by stroking them (purr/meow sound).

CATGRID PREMIUM (OPTIONAL SUBSCRIPTION)
Auto-renewable, monthly or yearly. Removes ads and unlocks 9 Golden Puzzles per breed plus Golden Cards. Open it from the home screen banner, from Settings → Premium, or from the "Golden Puzzles" teaser at the bottom of a breed's level screen. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are in Settings → Premium. As a free sample, the Golden Puzzles of British Shorthair unlock for everyone once that breed is finished.

ADS
Google AdMob. Interstitials appear only between puzzles (after every 2 solved puzzles, at least 90 seconds apart), never during play, never in tutorials. Rewarded ads are optional (extra paw or extra time after losing). In the EEA/UK the Google consent form appears on first launch, followed by the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
