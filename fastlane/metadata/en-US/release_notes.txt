Three new game modes!

• Meow Crush: match cat treats in 300 levels across the Cat Tower
• Meow Sort: sort the birds on their branches in 450 levels across six islands
• Cat Block: fill the 8×8 board with blocks and chase your best score
• Pause and resume: games continue where you left off
• New Game Center leaderboards for the new modes
• A new launch animation, faster start-up and many small improvements
