Fill the grid, follow the numbers and watch a hidden cat picture appear!

Catgrid Collection is a cozy nonogram (griddlers, hanjie) game made for cat lovers. Every puzzle can be solved with pure logic, so you never have to guess.

26 CAT BREEDS TO MEET
From the Siamese and Persian to the Maine Coon, Turkish Van and Japanese Bobtail. Each breed has 16 puzzles that grow from easy 5×5 grids to tricky 12×12 challenges.

COLLECT BREED CARDS
Finish a breed to win its collectible card with origin, lifespan, coat, personality stats and a fun fact. Flip the card to read more about the breed.

CAT PUZZLE
Place one cat in every color, row and column, and no two cats may touch. 335 handcrafted levels across four seasonal adventure maps, from spring gardens to winter tales. Easy mode crosses out the cells around every cat you find; Hard mode leaves it all to you.

NEW: MEOW CRUSH
Match three or more cat treats, create striped yarns and power-ups, and complete each level's goals. 300 levels across the four buildings of the Cat Tower, every room full of sleepy cats.

NEW: MEOW SORT
Sort the birds so every branch holds a single kind. When a branch is complete, the birds fly away and the little cat below cheers. 450 levels across six islands.

NEW: CAT BLOCK
Place pastel blocks on an 8×8 board and clear full rows and columns. Chain combos, sweep the board clean and beat your best score.

Pause any time: Meow Crush, Meow Sort and Cat Block continue right where you left off.

DAILY PUZZLE
A fresh expert nonogram every day. Keep your streak, catch up on missed days and earn weekly badges.

SHARE AND COMPETE
Share your solved puzzles with friends and climb the Game Center leaderboards.

LEARN IN MINUTES
Kitten School teaches every technique step by step in 11 short lessons.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Easy mode crosses out finished lines for you, Hard mode leaves every X to you
• Fill, cross and undo tools
• A little cat helper who points to a line with a sure move when you're stuck
• Your progress is saved automatically

COZY DETAILS
Calming music and sound effects, four color palettes with light and dark mode, haptics and 8 languages.
